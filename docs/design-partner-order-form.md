## Thirty-day evidence plan

The parties will attempt one customer-approved stale-head scenario, wrong-publisher scenario, and protected-evidence-change scenario without using production secrets or destructive customer data. ChangePlane will measure activation time, terminal Guard latency, false blocks, stuck Guards, support effort, and customer-confirmed value. The experiment cannot authorize ChangePlane or a model to merge code.

## Signatures

This order form becomes effective only when signed by the individual customer or authorized business representative and the provider's authorized representative, and incorporated into legally approved Design Partner Terms. Electronic acceptance in the product is not implemented in this candidate.
