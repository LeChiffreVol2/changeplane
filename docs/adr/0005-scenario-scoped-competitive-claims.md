# Require scenario-scoped evidence for competitive claims

ChangePlane will call a competitive result a win only when a customer bake-off or independent evaluation measures the same assurance scenario against a named alternative. The open benchmark covers stale heads, wrong publishers, evidence tampering, protected paths, same-revision reruns, provider failure, malformed or expanded patches, Merge Queue isolation, false decisions, activation time, decision overhead, cost per protected repository, and human interventions. Vendor-run results remain labeled vendor-run and never become a general superiority claim.

## Consequences

Feature count and broad “beats Origin” language are not success measures. Public claims identify the tested product version, scenario, configuration, date, evidence source, and known boundary. Missing competitor access is recorded as missing evidence rather than scored as a failure.
