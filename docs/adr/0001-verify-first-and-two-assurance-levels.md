# Sell Verify first with two explicit assurance levels

ChangePlane will sell Verify as the core product for GitHub organizations and keep Autonomous Repair as a controlled expansion until at least three paying customer organizations request it. The product will expose Strict Head for exact-revision enforcement without Merge Queue and Queue Certified for the stronger Merge Queue contract, preserving an honest boundary instead of excluding the initial startup market or weakening the highest-assurance claim. ChangePlane competes as independent behavioral merge assurance, not as a general agentic SDLC platform or Cursor Origin replacement.

## Consequences

Personal repositories are evaluation surfaces rather than the paid ICP. Product copy, readiness, onboarding, receipts, and pricing must name the assurance level explicitly and must never present Strict Head as Merge Queue proof.
