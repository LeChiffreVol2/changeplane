# Acceptable Use Policy — approval draft

This draft concerns the ChangePlane-operated hosted service. Use of the open-source software is governed by [Apache-2.0](LICENSE); the CLI and read-only GitHub Action do not call that service.

**Status:** Not yet effective. This draft must be approved with the Design Partner Terms for one exact protected Production release.

Customers and users must not use ChangePlane to:

- access, test, modify, or disrupt a repository, account, system, or data without authorization;
- introduce malware, credential theft, destructive payloads, denial-of-service traffic, or supply-chain attacks;
- evade GitHub policy, falsify evidence, impersonate a publisher, or cause an authoring agent or model to obtain Guard, approval, merge, or credential authority;
- place secrets, regulated production data, special-category personal data, payment-card data, health data, precise location, or unnecessary personal information in prompts, comments, evidence, configuration, or support reports;
- violate law, sanctions, export controls, intellectual-property, privacy, confidentiality, employment, or contractual rights;
- use the service for weapons, critical infrastructure control, medical diagnosis, emergency dispatch, or another safety-critical decision where a failed or delayed result could cause serious harm; or
- probe or bypass rate limits, quotas, access controls, protected paths, campaign limits, audit boundaries, or fail-closed behavior.

ChangePlane may fail closed, restrict, or suspend use when it reasonably detects abuse, unsafe authority, ambiguous evidence, platform risk, or a material violation. Security research must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not test against customer repositories or production without written authorization.
